The owner's exact grant now permits the new Lean contributions under Apache-2.0 to the extent the owner may license them; see the [scope and historical-label explanation](docs/releases/v0.6.2-source-permission.md). Regular private source admission is recorded separately. Publication events are recorded separately in the release assets.

Lean, Lake, Mathlib and transitive packages use their respective upstream licenses. Revisions are recorded in [the dependency lock](lake-manifest.json). See [Lip source attribution](docs/lip-fix2/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for distributed ports and proof-route adaptations.

Source and final declaration identities, file hashes and change descriptions accompany the [attribution record](docs/qualification/naimark-vn/adaptations.json) and [notices](docs/qualification/naimark-vn/W01-A-NOTICES.md).
